KSL Live Transcriber

Live hand-sign recognition trained by you, transcribing straight into Kazakh text — no translation step needed, since the signs already are Kazakh.

Phase 1 of 3 · trainable prototype
Where this fits in the bigger plan
01 — now

Trainable transcriber

Alphabet, digits, and essential words — plus any word you add yourself. You record a few examples of each sign; a nearest-neighbor matcher recognizes them live and builds a Kazakh transcript. Every recording is a labeled data point.

02 — next

Corpus from news footage

Run speech-to-text on KSL-interpreted news video, then align the spoken transcript to the interpreter's signs with human review — turning hours of footage into a large labeled sign↔word database. See docs/roadmap.md.

03 — later

Two-way voiceover

A model trained on that database reads signs aloud as speech, and speech back into signs, so a hearing person and a KSL signer can talk directly, in both directions.
